Research

With research on, INDAI also reads today's news, events and sentiment on the web before rating a trade. The analysis is information, not financial advice.

TradingView

INDAI Bridge streams your TradingView candles into INDAI every minute, so charts and setup alerts run on your live TradingView data instead of 15-minute-delayed data. Add the INDAI Bridge indicator to a 1-minute chart and create one alert with your INDAI webhook link. Any other TradingView alert sent to the same link is read out loud, and alerts with levels like CW 700 PW 650 ZG 676 update the GEX lines. Webhooks need a paid TradingView plan with two-factor authentication on.
